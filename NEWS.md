## LEAD — COP31 collapses (Antalya, Turkey)
- UN climate talks collapsed on Sunday after more than two weeks; the longest COP in
  history and the first major breakdown since COP15 in Copenhagen in 2009.
- The final "Antalya Pact" draft never mentioned fossil fuels. The EU, Panama, the
  Marshall Islands and Norway rejected it. Ireland's climate minister Darragh O'Brien
  (EU presidency): the bloc "will not endorse a deal that does not even mention
  fossil fuels".
- COP31 president Murat Kurum at the closing plenary: "It is clear that there are
  parties in this hall who oppose this text. We recognize that there is no consensus
  on this document."
- The US was not among the parties: under President Trump it has pulled out of
  international climate action. 90+ countries signed a pledge to ramp up
  electrification (Chris Bowen, COP31 president of negotiations).
- Sources: CNN 22 Nov; WSJ; Le Monde 22 Nov; Nikkei 22 Nov 23:05 JST; Al Jazeera
  22 Nov.

## GRID 1 — HORMUZ: three tankers hit
- Three tankers struck exiting the Strait of Hormuz on Friday and Saturday: the
  Captain Leon (Marshall Islands-flagged, bow, Fri 15:00 UTC, fire aboard), the
  Torkel (Hong Kong-flagged crude, stern, Sat 05:00 UTC, fire), the Arina
  (Hong Kong-flagged, engine room, missile, <1 h later). No casualties reported.
- Iran's IRGC navy claimed on Sunday that two supertankers hit naval mines and
  "exploded and were stopped" near the strait; one was said to carry 2m+ barrels
  (Iranian claim, unverified; no names, flags or crew details given).
- CNN analysis of UKMTO data: at least 22 attacks reported in November as of Sunday,
  the second-highest monthly figure since the US–Israel–Iran war began in late
  February (31 in October).
- Bahrain: an Iranian attack set fire to a civilian industrial facility on Saturday;
  on Sunday Manama took the matter to the UN Security Council, demanding action and
  compensation. Iran says it will send 14 oil tankers through the US naval blockade.
- Sources: CNN 22 Nov; UKMTO via Arab News / AP; Times Now liveblog 23 Nov;
  Gulf News 22 Nov.

## GRID 2 — UKRAINE: 14 missing in Kyiv region
- A Russian drone struck a five-storey apartment building in Dymer, Kyiv region, on
  Sunday morning; part of the building collapsed. Nine people were evacuated, the
  fire was contained, and 14 residents were unaccounted for (President Zelenskyy).
- This week Russia attacked 20 regions of Ukraine, launching about 2,700 drones,
  almost 1,460 aerial bombs and 165 missiles (Zelenskyy).
- Sources: Reuters 22 Nov; UATV; Jerusalem Post 22 Nov; The Hindu 22 Nov.

## GRID 3 — NVIDIA: spy probe widens
- US Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton (Sunday TV): the US is
  investigating alleged Chinese spy Alice Huang, a former executive of
  Singapore-based Megaspeed who had an affair with former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang.
- Wall Street Journal: Chinese national Li Ming — on bail in Singapore, charged with
  fraud in Feb 2025 — ran a network of companies smuggling restricted Nvidia AI
  chips to China, including Megaspeed, where Alice Huang was a managing director.
  Megaspeed is suspected of moving close to $12bn of chips between Mar 2024 and
  Jun 2025 despite export controls.
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent (Sunday): the US is seeking answers from Nvidia
  on its chip sales to China and will find out who was in charge of compliance.
- Jensen Huang is to face questions from lawmakers on 1 December (House
  Intelligence, Foreign Affairs and China committees). The SEC is probing Nvidia's
  accounting (reported 20 Nov); California's attorney general served an
  investigative subpoena on 20 Nov.
- Sources: Japan Times 23 Nov; Reuters 22 Nov; WSJ via TBS News / The Times;
  The Outpost.

## GRID 4 — INDONESIA: 3 dead, 36 missing in factory fire
- A fire swept through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory (Milkita brand) in
  Cikande, Serang, Banten province, on Saturday at 17:00 local time. About 300
  workers were inside; 15 were rescued alive (four injured); three were killed and
  36 remained unaccounted for on Sunday (BASARNAS). The remains of the three killed
  were held in seven body bags for DNA identification.
- Sources: CNA; Al Jazeera 22 Nov; AP via Bernama; New Indian Express 22 Nov.
